Privacy Policy

BahirUpload · Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Who we are

BahirUpload ("the app") is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel @bahir96. The only person who signs in to and uses the app is the channel owner.

2. Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests two permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to the owner's channel and to set their visibility.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the signed-in channel's ID and name, to make sure uploads go to the correct channel, and to read the processing status of videos the app uploaded.

The app does not access, collect or use any other YouTube or Google account data: no watch history, subscriptions, comments, contacts, email or analytics.

4. How the information is used and stored

  • The channel ID, channel name and video status are used only at the moment of upload, to check the channel and confirm the upload succeeded. They are not saved.
  • The Google sign-in (OAuth) token is stored only on the owner's own computer, so the app can upload without asking the owner to sign in each time. It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The app has no server and no database. It does not keep any YouTube API Data beyond the stored sign-in token.

5. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any user information with anyone, internally or externally. Data is exchanged only between the owner's computer and Google/YouTube, to perform the upload.

6. Third-party content and advertising

The app does not show advertising, and no third parties serve content through it.

7. Cookies and device storage

The app does not use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics. The only thing it stores on a device is the sign-in token described in section 4, on the owner's own computer. No third party stores or collects information on users' devices through the app.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from Google's security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Revoking access makes the stored token useless immediately. To delete it completely, delete the token file from the computer where the app runs. Because the app keeps no other data, nothing else remains to delete. You can also ask us by email to confirm deletion, and we will respond within 7 days.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
