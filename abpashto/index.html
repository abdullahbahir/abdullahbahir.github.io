New videos every day

Three new Pashto videos are published every day, at times that suit our viewers in Afghanistan:

Afghanistan timeWhat you'll see
11:00A useful, practical tech story
17:00The most surprising news of the day
20:00The biggest, most visual story

Every story is checked in at least two trusted news sources before we make a video, and each video ends with a question so viewers can share their ideas.

How we make our videos

  1. Research: we read the latest technology news from the past one or two days.
  2. Check: we confirm the facts in at least two reputable sources and skip rumors.
  3. Write: we write a short script in natural Afghan Pashto, with numbers explained simply.
  4. Produce: the narration uses an AI Pashto voice, with free-to-use stock footage that is labeled when it only illustrates the story.
  5. Publish: we post the video to our own TikTok and Facebook accounts with our app, BahirPashtoUpload.
