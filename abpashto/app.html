BahirPashtoUpload

BahirPashtoUpload is the publishing tool made by Bahir Media for abpashto. It posts the Pashto videos we make to our own TikTok account, @abpashto.

Open the app

What it does

Sign in with TikTok

The account owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit and approves access. We only read the basic profile (name and avatar) to show which account is signed in.

Choose and review

The owner picks one of their finished videos, writes the caption, and chooses who can see it. There is no default privacy setting.

Post to TikTok

The video is uploaded with TikTok's Content Posting API, and the app shows when TikTok has finished processing it.

How it works, step by step

  1. Open the app page and click Sign in with TikTok.
  2. Approve the permissions on TikTok's own page.
  3. Pick a video, check the preview, write the caption and choose the privacy level.
  4. Set the interaction and content-disclosure options, read the consent line, and click Post.
  5. The status updates until TikTok confirms the post.

Who can use it

BahirPashtoUpload is used only by the owner of abpashto to publish videos they created. It is not a public service and has no other users. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. Sign-in tokens stay on the owner's own computer.
