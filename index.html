BahirShortUpload

BahirShortUpload is a personal, non-commercial tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel @bahir96 to upload short videos that the owner created to that same channel.

It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and to confirm which channel it is signed in to. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Its companion tool BahirPashtoUpload posts the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok account @abpashto using TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API.

By using BahirShortUpload you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
